Patwa Wave — iPhone / Mac text replacements (Jamaican Patois)
This is NOT a third-party keyboard app. It uses Apple Text Replacement.
Type a shortcut like /hello and iPhone expands it to Wah gwaan in any app.
No extra permissions. No network. Works with your normal Apple keyboard.

Official Mac import: System Settings → Keyboard → Text Replacements → drag this .plist in.
Then iCloud syncs to your iPhone (same Apple Account + iCloud Drive).
Apple docs: https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchl2a7bd795/mac
iPhone text replacement help: https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph6d01d862/ios
